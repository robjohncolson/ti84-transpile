# Task: Decode ROM functions at 0x07D189 and 0x07D183 — Matrix/List Sign Special Handlers

## Context

## What we know about 0x07D189 and 0x07D183
- Called from the OP1/OP2 sign toggle pair (session 531):

  - 0x07CA27 (OP2 sign toggle): `CP 0x1D → JP Z,0x07D183` (list type special handling)
- Wait — actually checking session 531 more carefully:
  - 0x07CA02: `CP 0x1C; JR Z,skip; CP 0x1D → JP Z,0x07D189` (matrix skip, list→0x07D189)
  - 0x07CA27: `CP 0x1C; JR Z,skip; CP 0x1D → JP Z,0x07D183` (matrix skip, list→0x07D183)
- So 0x07D189 handles list (0x1D) sign toggle for OP1, and 0x07D183 handles list sign toggle for OP2
- These are 6 bytes apart (0x07D183 to 0x07D189), so they may share code
- Located in the matrix/list FP region (0x07D27B is the big 519B stat computation nearby)
