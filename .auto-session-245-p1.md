NZ path: EI, LD A,(D02A86), CP 0x00 → Z=JP 0x027111, NZ=DEC A→LD (D02A86),A→JP 0x027123. These are the two JP targets when BIT 3,(IY+9) is set. We need to know: what do 0x027111 and 0x027123 do?
