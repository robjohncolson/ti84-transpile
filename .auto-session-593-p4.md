- LD (nn),rp / LD rp,(nn) = 5 bytes (ED prefix)
- .SIS prefix (0x40/0x49/0x52/0x5B) switches to 16-bit immediates
- IY-prefixed instructions use FD prefix: FD CB dd op for BIT/SET/RES on (IY+d)
