# Task: Investigate why D00596 jumps to 0x13 (19) when calling 0x0059C6 with D00595=0

## Context
You are working on a TI-84 Plus CE ROM transpilation project. The ROM is lifted to JavaScript.

Session 481 found that calling 0x0059C6 with A=0x48 ('H'), D00595=0, D00596=0 causes:
- D00596 jumps from 0 to 0x13 (19)
- 147,538 VRAM bytes changed (nearly full framebuffer)
- Function doesn't return in 50K steps
- D00595 advances to 0x04, D005A0=0x85 (133)

The hypothesis is that 0x0059C6 with D00595=0 and D00596=0 enters a screen fill path through 0x005A20 (padding) that iterates through the mode-text buffer at D020A6 ("Normal Float Radian       " = 26 chars). The "column stuck at 0x13" may mean it rendered 19 columns of something before our step budget ran out.
