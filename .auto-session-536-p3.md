## eZ80 decoder notes
- Standard Z80 opcodes. CB/ED/DD/FD prefixes.
- CALL nn / JP nn: 3-byte little-endian address
- LD rr,nn: 3-byte immediate in eZ80
- LD A,(nn) = 0x3A, LD (nn),A = 0x32: 3-byte address
- JR: signed 8-bit displacement from byte after JR
- FD CB dd op: IY bit ops (displacement then opcode)
