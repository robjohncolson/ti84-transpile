## Task
Compare two utility functions in the TI-84 Plus CE ROM: `cpHL_DE_modeDependent` at 0x04C960 (134 callers) and `cpHL_DE` at 0x04C979 (252 callers). They are both HL vs DE compare utilities in the utility family region 0x04C800-0x04CA40.

## What to do

1. **Disassemble both functions** from the ROM binary at `TI-84_Plus_CE/ROM.rom`. The ROM is eZ80 (ADL mode by default, 24-bit addresses). Use the transpiled JS as reference: `TI-84_Plus_CE/ROM.transpiled.js` (gunzip from `.gz` first if needed, or read the `.gz`).

2. **Find all callers of each function.** Search `ROM.transpiled.js` for:
   - References to `0x04C960` or `sub_04C960` (cpHL_DE_modeDependent)
   - References to `0x04C979` or `sub_04C979` (cpHL_DE)
   
3. **Analyze the difference.** The hypothesis is that one is ADL-mode aware (checks MBASE or uses different instruction widths) while the other is a fixed-width compare. Key questions:
   - What instructions differ between them?
   - Does one check CPU mode (ADL bit, MBASE register)?
   - Do callers from different ROM regions prefer one over the other?
   - Group callers by ROM region (0x00xxxx, 0x01xxxx, 0x02xxxx, etc.) and see if there's a pattern.

4. **Write results** to `TI-84_Plus_CE/phase312-cpHL-DE-comparison-report.md` with:
   - Full disassembly of both functions
   - Caller count and distribution by ROM region
   - Explanation of when/why callers choose one vs the other
   - Any register contracts (inputs/outputs/clobbers)

5. **Write a probe** at `TI-84_Plus_CE/probe-phase312-cpHL-DE-comparison.mjs` that:
   - Loads the ROM
   - Reads bytes at both addresses
   - Counts CALL references to each in the transpiled JS
