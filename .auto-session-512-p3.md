## What we know

- 0x0B8516 and 0x0B8525 are two of those call sites, located inside 0x0B852A (cursor_pair_iter, decoded in session 510)
- 0x0B852A (92 bytes) iterates 3 cursor rows writing D00595 = 0/1/2, calling 0x0BBF0D and 0x0B9032
- 0x0B8516 and 0x0B8525 are BEFORE 0x0B852A, suggesting they're in a separate function (possibly 0x0B8505 which is called from phase 1 of cursor_pair_iter)
- Other cursor-area rasterizer call sites: 0x0B904A, 0x0B9051, 0x096C80, 0x096CB1, 0x096FC3
- The rasterizer 0x0A1799 takes: HL=glyph data pointer, DE=VRAM dest, B=height, C=width (likely)
- Font table at ROM 0x003D6E, 28 bytes/glyph, copied to RAM D005A1
- Y = row × 20 + 37 (from 0x0A2D4C), X = col × 12 + 2 (from 0x00038C)
