These two functions were discovered in session 420 as callees of 0x0094C0 (the legacy TI-Link ISR, 523 bytes):
- 0x00ED77 = handshake handler — called during the TI-Link serial communication handshake

0x0094C0 is the legacy TI-Link serial ISR that polls D14049 (shadow of port 0x3014) and dispatches to 6 interrupt bit handlers. It uses port 0x30xx range. The TI-Link protocol uses a custom serial protocol over 2 data lines.

Other known callees of 0x0094C0: 0x00DA8C (disconnect handler).
