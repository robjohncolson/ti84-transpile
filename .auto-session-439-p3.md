## eZ80 Encoding Notes
- eZ80 is little-endian, 24-bit addresses
- `LD A,(D14080)` = `3A 80 40 D1`
- `LD (D14080),A` = `32 80 40 D1`
- `LD A,(D140B2)` = `3A B2 40 D1`
- `LD (D140B2),A` = `32 B2 40 D1`
- `LD A,(D14082)` = `3A 82 40 D1`
- `LD (D14082),A` = `32 82 40 D1`
- `XOR A` (AF) before store = writing 0x00
- `OR A` (B7) after load = zero-test
- ROM banks mirror: 0x00xxxx, 0x02xxxx, 0x04xxxx contain duplicated code
