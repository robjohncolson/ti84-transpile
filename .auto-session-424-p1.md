## Background

Session 423 identified a slab allocator function at approximately 0x00E06D, estimated ~641 bytes. This is the counterpart to the slab FREE function at 0x00E1CC (287 bytes, decoded in session 422). Together they form a complete slab allocator:
- **Init**: 0x00CB29 (writes D1401A/D1401D/D14020)
- **Alloc**: ~0x00E06D (~641 bytes) — THIS IS YOUR TARGET
- **Free**: 0x00E1CC (287 bytes, selector-driven: selector 0 frees 0x20-byte slab from D1401A-D1401D pool, selector 2 frees 0x400-byte slab from D14020 pool via D1406C bitmap)

Key RAM variables:
- D1401A: lower bound of 0x20-byte slab pool
- D1401D: upper bound of 0x20-byte slab pool
- D14020: base address of 0x400-byte slab pool
- D1406C: free bitmap for 0x400-byte pool (6 entries)
- D1400B: current block pointer (43 ROM refs, most referenced slab variable)
