# Auto-Session 312 — Priority 3: Trace Absent Certificate Fields

## Task
The certificate region at 0x3B0001-0x3C0000 was fully decoded in session 311. It contains 6 TLV fields. But callers request 7 distinct field types, and 3 of those (0x0C10, 0x0300, 0x0230) are ABSENT from the certificate data. Trace what happens when FindFirstCertField fails to find these absent fields.

- ABSENT field types: 0x0C10, 0x0300, 0x0230
- Previous report: `TI-84_Plus_CE/phase311-cert-region-report.md`
