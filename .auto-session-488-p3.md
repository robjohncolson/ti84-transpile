## How to Verify CODE? Ranges
Look at the bytes. Valid eZ80 code typically:
- Starts with common opcodes: 0xCD (CALL), 0xC3 (JP), 0x21 (LD HL,nn), 0x3E (LD A,n), 0xED prefix instructions
- Contains CALL targets (0xCD xx xx xx) that point to known ROM addresses (< 0x400000)
- Does NOT look like ASCII strings (sequences of 0x20-0x7E) or pointer tables (repeating 3-byte patterns)
