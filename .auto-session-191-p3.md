## Context

Session 190 identified that entering at ~0x05848F (past the 0x06EDFE and 0x083623/0x083764 setup calls) should bypass the stall and reach key dispatch.

- Session 184 finding: Copy helper 0x07F9FB copies 9 bytes from TOKEN_STAGING (0xD0230E) → OP1 before classification
